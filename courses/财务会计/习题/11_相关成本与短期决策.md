# Relevant Costs and Short-Term Decisions Exercises

## Special Order 1

Georgia sells soft toys for $30 each. Unit cost consists of direct materials of $10, direct labor of $6, variable manufacturing overhead of $4, and allocated fixed manufacturing overhead of $6. A customer offers $25 each for a special order of 400 units. Special-purpose tools costing $600 must be rented. Assuming idle capacity, by how much would accepting the order increase or decrease profit?

## Special Order 2

A science museum charges $6 admission and has daily capacity for 500 visitors, but current attendance is no more than 53. Fixed cost per visitor is stated as $3 and variable cost per visitor is $0.50. A school offers $2 per ticket for 25 tickets. Assuming idle capacity, what is the effect on net profit?

## Judgment Questions

1. Which item is irrelevant to a special-order decision: sunk cost, qualitative factors, idle capacity, or variable cost?
2. Under what condition is discontinuing a loss-making product line more likely to increase profit?
3. Is allocated and unavoidable fixed cost relevant to a make-or-buy decision?

## Answers and Explanations

Special Order 1: incremental revenue = 400 × $25 = $10,000; incremental variable cost = 400 × ($10 + $6 + $4) = $8,000; special tools = $600; profit increases by $1,400. Allocated fixed manufacturing overhead is irrelevant.

Special Order 2: unit incremental contribution = $2 - $0.50 = $1.50; net profit increases by 25 × $1.50 = $37.50.

Judgment questions: 1 sunk cost; 2 when avoidable fixed costs exceed the contribution margin lost plus any other opportunity costs; 3 no.
