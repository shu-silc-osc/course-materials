# Flexible Budgeting and Variance Analysis Exercises

## Yoshino Variance Analysis

Actual sales volume is 3,560 units. Flexible-budget and actual results are as follows: sales revenue 178,000/170,000; direct materials 7,120/12,000; direct labor 10,680/12,000; variable manufacturing overhead 356/300; fixed manufacturing overhead 3,000/2,000; variable selling and administrative expense 1,780/3,000; fixed selling and administrative expense 5,000/20,000.

Select the three largest individual flexible-budget variances and, for each:

1. State the variance name and amount.
2. Classify it as favorable or unfavorable.
3. Analyze possible causes.
4. Recommend corrective action or identify additional information required.

## Answer Guide

The three largest variances are: fixed selling and administrative expense, 15,000 unfavorable; sales revenue, 8,000 unfavorable; and direct materials, 4,880 unfavorable.

Possible causes include unbudgeted advertising or administrative expenditure, a lower actual selling price or heavier discounts, higher material prices, or inefficient material usage. Separate price and quantity effects and review discount policy, purchase prices, scrap rates, and one-time expenditure.
