# Management Accounting and Decision Information Exercises

## Multiple-Choice and Classification Questions

1. The primary focus of management accounting is: A. External reporting B. Decision support C. Tax filing D. Audit opinions.
2. Which of the following are normally external users: investors, shareholders, tax authorities, the CEO, sales managers, lenders, suppliers, and customers?
3. Classify each characteristic as financial accounting or management accounting:
   - It does not have to follow a uniform reporting format.
   - It provides more disaggregated information.
   - It primarily serves internal users.
   - It places greater emphasis on the future.
4. What is usually the first stage of the sustainability reporting cycle?

## Answers

1. B.
2. Investors, shareholders, tax authorities, lenders, suppliers, and customers.
3. All four characteristics are more consistent with management accounting.
4. Planning.
