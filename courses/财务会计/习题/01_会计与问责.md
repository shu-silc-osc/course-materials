# Accounting and Accountability Exercises

## Concept Questions

1. What is accounting?
2. What is accountability?
3. How does accounting support accountability?
4. Which statements are consistent with utilitarianism?
   - A. The best decision should produce the greatest benefit for the greatest number of people.
   - B. The consequences of a decision are central to the ethical assessment.
   - C. Any action that complies with the law is necessarily ethical.
5. In a sole proprietorship, partnership, and corporation, to whom are managers primarily accountable?

## Answers and Explanations

1. Accounting identifies, measures, records, and reports economic events to support decision-making and accountability.
2. Accountability means accepting responsibility for one's decisions and actions and being able to explain them to relevant parties.
3. Accounting reports connect resource use, operating results, and managerial responsibility, enabling stakeholders to monitor and evaluate performance.
4. A and B.
5. A sole proprietor is primarily accountable to the owner and relevant stakeholders; partners are accountable to one another; corporate management is accountable to shareholders, while the corporation also has responsibilities to a broader group of stakeholders.
