# Budgeting and the Master Budget Exercises

## Multiple-Choice Questions

1. An annual budget normally covers: A. One year B. Two years C. Three years D. Four years.
2. What consequences may arise when employee bonuses are tied directly to sales targets?
3. Which budget requires information about when customer receivables will be collected: the sales budget, budgeted balance sheet, production budget, or cash budget?

## Calculation

A manufacturer expects first-quarter sales of 1,000 units, desired ending finished goods of 150 units, and beginning finished goods of 100 units. Each unit requires 3 kilograms of material. Desired ending material inventory is 400 kilograms, beginning material inventory is 250 kilograms, and material costs ¥8 per kilogram.

1. Calculate budgeted production.
2. Calculate material purchases in kilograms and monetary terms.

## Answers and Explanations

Multiple choice: 1 A; 2 the incentive may encourage sales at any cost, weaken attention to customer satisfaction, and create spending variances, so performance should be evaluated using balanced measures; 3 the cash budget.

Budgeted production = 1,000 + 150 - 100 = 1,050 units. Material required for production = 1,050 × 3 = 3,150 kilograms. Material purchases = 3,150 + 400 - 250 = 3,300 kilograms. Purchase cost = 3,300 × ¥8 = ¥26,400.
