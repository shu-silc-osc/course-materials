# Transaction Recording and Account Changes Exercises

## Ending Accounts Receivable

A business begins the period with accounts receivable of $18,000. During the period:

1. It collects $1,540 of receivables.
2. It makes a credit sale of $5,082.
3. It makes cash sales of $25,000.
4. It records another $5,000 sale, but the customer had paid in advance.
5. It collects half of the receivable created in item 2.

Calculate ending accounts receivable.

## Accrued Wages

At period-end, an employee has worked 15 hours at $24 per hour but has not yet been paid. In the next period, the employee works another 16 hours and is then paid for all hours. Explain the effects on cash, wages payable, and wages expense at period-end and on the payment date.

## Answers and Explanations

Ending accounts receivable = $18,000 - $1,540 + $5,082 - $2,541 = $19,001. A sale paid in advance affects cash and a contract liability before performance; it becomes revenue upon performance and does not create a receivable.

At period-end, wages payable and wages expense each increase by $360. Upon payment, cash decreases by $744, wages payable decreases by $360, and wages expense for the new period increases by $384.
