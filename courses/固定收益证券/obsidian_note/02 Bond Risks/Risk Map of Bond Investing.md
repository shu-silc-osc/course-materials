## 关系图

```
Interest Rate Risk
├─ affects [[Bond Price Volatility]]
├─ measured by [[Duration Overview]]
├─ improved by [[Convexity]]
└─ managed by [[Interest Rate Futures]]

Credit Risk
├─ reflected in [[Yield Spread]]
├─ linked to [[Credit Spread]]
└─ important for [[Corporate Debt Securities]] and [[ABS Asset Risk]]

Call Risk
├─ linked to [[Callable Bonds]]
├─ causes [[Price Compression]]
├─ causes [[Negative Convexity]]
└─ affects [[Yield to Call]] and [[Yield to Worst]]

Reinvestment Risk
├─ important for coupon bonds
├─ offsetting effect against interest-rate risk
└─ important in [[Total Return Horizon Yield]]
```
