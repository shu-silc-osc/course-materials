#### Comprehensive Questions

1. **[Key Question]** Answer each estimation problem.

(a) A random sample of 25 nominal 10 kg potato bags has $\bar x=10.52$ kg and $s^2=1.43$. Assuming normality, construct a 95% confidence interval for the population mean.

(b) A sample of 77 flights has mean delay 2.48 minutes; the population standard deviation is 12 minutes. Give the point estimate and a 95% confidence interval.

(c) In a random sample of 80 students, 15 use an iPad for class notes. Give the point estimate and, if conditions permit, a 99% confidence interval for the population proportion.

(d) Ten CEOs have mean annual compensation USD 10 million and sample standard deviation USD 15 million. The population is not known to be normal. Can the course method construct a 95% confidence interval?

> Difficulty: Advanced

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(a) With $t_{0.025,24}=2.064$, the interval is $10.52\pm2.064(\sqrt{1.43}/\sqrt{25})=[10.0264,11.0136]$ kg.

(b) Point estimate: 2.48 minutes. With $z_{0.025}=1.96$, the interval is $2.48\pm1.96(12/\sqrt{77})=[-0.2004,5.1604]$ minutes.

(c) $\hat p=15/80=0.1875$. Since $n\hat p=15$ and $n(1-\hat p)=65$, the normal conditions hold. With $z_{0.005}=2.576$, the interval is $[0.0751,0.2999]$.

(d) No. With $n=10$ and no normal-population assumption, the interval is outside the methods covered.

</details>
