#### Calculation and Application Questions

1. **[Key Question]** The number of accidents on an assembly line follows a Poisson distribution with an average of three accidents per week. Find:

(a) the probability that a week is accident-free;

(b) the probability of at least three accidents in a week;

(c) the probability of exactly five accidents in a week;

(d) the expected number of accidents in a year;

(e) the probability of at most four accidents in a fortnight.

> Difficulty: Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(a) $P(X=0)=0.0498$.

(b) $P(X\ge3)=1-P(X<3)=0.5768$.

(c) $P(X=5)=0.1008$.

(d) The annual mean is $3(52)=156$ accidents.

(e) For a fortnight, $\lambda=6$, so $P(X\le4)=0.2851$.

</details>

2. **[Key Question]** A shoe store finds that 30% of purchasing customers use a credit card. Twenty customers make purchases. Assuming a binomial model, find:

(a) the probability exactly 5 use a credit card;

(b) the probability at most 3 use a credit card;

(c) the probability from 3 through 6, inclusive, use a credit card;

(d) the expected number and standard deviation for customers using a credit card;

(e) the expected number and standard deviation for customers not using a credit card.

> Difficulty: Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

With $n=20$ and $p=0.3$:

(a) $P(X=5)=0.179$.

(b) $P(X\le3)=0.108$.

(c) $P(3\le X\le6)=0.573$.

(d) $E(X)=np=6$ and $\sigma=\sqrt{np(1-p)}=2.0494$.

(e) The complementary count has mean 14 and the same standard deviation, 2.0494.

</details>
