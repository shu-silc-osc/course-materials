1. **[Key Question]** A survey covers 40 stocks in the aerospace and food sectors. Nineteen are rated high risk. Of the 15 aerospace stocks, 6 are low risk. If one stock is selected at random, find the probability that it is:

(a) low risk;

(b) from the food sector and low risk;

(c) from the aerospace sector or high risk;

(d) low risk, given that it is from the aerospace sector.

> Difficulty: Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

The completed counts are: aerospace/high = 9, aerospace/low = 6, food/high = 10, and food/low = 15.

(a) $P(L)=21/40=0.525$.

(b) $P(F\cap L)=15/40=0.375$.

(c) $P(A\cup H)=15/40+19/40-9/40=0.625$.

(d) $P(L\mid A)=(6/40)/(15/40)=0.4$.

</details>
