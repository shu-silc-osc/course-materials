# Chapter 8: Sample-Size Determination

#### Calculation and Application Questions

1. **[Key Question]** Determine the minimum sample size in each case.

(a) Estimate mean rat weight gain within 2 g at 95% confidence when $\sigma=15$ g.

(b) Estimate the average appliance repair bill within USD 20 at 95% confidence. Bills are believed to range from USD 30 to USD 600; estimate $\sigma$ using the range rule.

(c) Estimate the proportion of sport utility vehicles in Melbourne rush-hour traffic with margin of error at most 0.03 and 90% confidence, using the prior belief $p\le0.40$.

> Difficulty: Advanced

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(a) $n=(1.96\times15/2)^2=216.09$; round up to 217.

(b) Estimate $\sigma=(600-30)/4=142.5$. Then $n=(1.96\times142.5/20)^2=195.0212$; round up to 196.

(c) Using $p=0.4$ and $q=0.6$, $n=1.645^2(0.4)(0.6)/0.03^2=721.6067$; round up to 722.
