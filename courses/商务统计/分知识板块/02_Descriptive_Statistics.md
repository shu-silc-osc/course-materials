#### Calculation and Application Questions

1. **[Key Question]** The numbers of new products introduced by five companies are 768, 429, 323, 306, and 286.

(a) Find the mean, mode, and median.

(b) Find the sample variance and sample standard deviation.

(c) Find the 60th and 20th percentiles using the course convention.

(d) Find the range and interquartile range.

(e) Find the z-score for 323.

(f) Find the coefficient of variation.

> Difficulty: Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(a) Mean = 422.4; no mode; median = 323.

(b) The sum of squared deviations is 161,517.2, so $s^2 = 161,517.2/(5-1) = 40,379.3$ and $s = 200.946$.

(c) $P_{60} = (323+429)/2 = 376$ and $P_{20} = (286+306)/2 = 296$.

(d) Range = $768-286=482$. Under the course percentile-position rule, $Q_1=306$ and $Q_3=429$, so IQR = 123.

(e) $z=(323-422.4)/200.946=-0.495$.

(f) $CV=200.946/422.4=0.4757=47.57\%$.

</details>

2. **[Key Question]** A distribution is approximately bell-shaped, with mean 125 and standard deviation 12. Use the Empirical Rule to determine:

(a) the approximate proportion of values greater than 137;

(b) the approximate proportion between 113 and 149;

(c) the interval containing approximately 95% of the values.

> Difficulty: Basic

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(a) Since 137 is one standard deviation above the mean, the proportion is approximately $(1-0.68)/2=0.16$, or 16%.

(b) The interval runs from one standard deviation below the mean to two above it, so the proportion is approximately $0.68/2+0.95/2=0.815$, or 81.5%.

(c) Approximately 95% lie within two standard deviations: $125\pm2(12)$, or from 101 to 149.

</details>

3. **[Key Question]** A distribution has mean 38 and standard deviation 6, but it is not normally distributed.

(a) What minimum proportion of values falls between 26 and 50, and which theorem justifies the result?

(b) Between what two values will at least 89% of the observations fall?

> Difficulty: Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(a) The endpoints are two standard deviations from the mean. Chebyshev's theorem gives at least $1-1/2^2=0.75$, or 75%.

(b) At least 89% corresponds to $k=3$. The interval is $38\pm3(6)$, or 20 to 56.

</details>
