#### Multiple-Choice Questions

1. **[Key Question]** A student achieves 82 on the first test in a statistics course. From this, she assumes that her average at the end of the semester, after other tests, will be about 82. This is an example of:

A) descriptive statistics

B) inferential statistics

C) nonparametric statistics

D) wishful thinking

> Difficulty: Basic

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

B

</details>

2. **[Key Question]** The Jacaranda Swimming Pool Company wants to estimate the average number of years before a major repair is required for all its pools. The company randomly contacts 50 families that purchased pools in the past ten years. The average based on those 50 families is best described as a:

A) statistic

B) sample

C) population

D) parameter

> Difficulty: Basic

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

A

</details>

3. **[Key Question]** A financial analyst needs a sample of 100 securities from an alphabetical list of 2,531 securities. He uses a random-number table to select 100 numbers between 1 and 2,531. His sample is a:

A) simple random sample

B) stratified sample

C) systematic sample

D) quota sample

> Difficulty: Basic

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

A

</details>
