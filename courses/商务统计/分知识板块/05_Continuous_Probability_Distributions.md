#### Calculation and Application Questions

1. **[Key Question]** A continuous uniform random variable ranges from 8 to 21.

(a) Find the density $f(x)$.

(b) Find its mean and standard deviation.

(c) Find $P(10\le X<17)$.

(d) Find $P(4\le X\le6)$.

> Difficulty: Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(a) $f(x)=1/(21-8)=1/13$ for $8\le x\le21$, and 0 otherwise.

(b) $\mu=(8+21)/2=14.5$ and $\sigma=(21-8)/\sqrt{12}=3.7528$.

(c) $P(10\le X<17)=(17-10)/(21-8)=0.5385$.

(d) The interval is outside the support, so the probability is 0.

</details>

2. **[Key Question]** Chemists' salaries are normally distributed with mean NZ$55,000 and standard deviation NZ$1,500. Find:

(a) $P(X<50,000)$;

(b) $P(X>57,000)$;

(c) $P(52,000\le X\le56,500)$;

(d) $P(X>51,000)$;

(e) the salary below which 90% of new chemistry graduates will be offered.

> Difficulty: Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(a) $z=-3.33$, so $P(X<50,000)=0.0004$.

(b) $z=1.33$, so $P(X>57,000)=0.0918$.

(c) The z-scores are -2 and 1, so the probability is $0.4772+0.3413=0.8185$.

(d) $z=-2.67$, so $P(X>51,000)=0.9962$.

(e) The 90th-percentile z-score is 1.28. Thus $x=55,000+1.28(1,500)=56,920$.

</details>
