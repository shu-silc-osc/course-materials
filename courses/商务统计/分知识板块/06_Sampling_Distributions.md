# Chapter 6: Sampling Distributions

#### Calculation and Application Questions

1. **[Key Question]** Adult Australian male weights have mean 70.3 kg and standard deviation 2.74 kg.

(a) Find the mean and standard deviation of the sampling distribution of the sample mean for samples of 36. State the theorem used.

(b) For a random sample of 40, find $P(\bar X>71)$.

(c) For a random sample of 25, can $P(\bar X<72)$ be determined using the methods covered if the population distribution is unknown?

> Difficulty: Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(a) By the Central Limit Theorem, $\mu_{\bar X}=70.3$ and $\sigma_{\bar X}=2.74/\sqrt{36}=0.4567$.

(b) $z=(71-70.3)/(2.74/\sqrt{40})=1.62$, so $P(\bar X>71)=0.0526$.

(c) No. The population is not known to be normal and $n=25<30$, so the normal sampling approximation used in the course is not justified.

</details>
