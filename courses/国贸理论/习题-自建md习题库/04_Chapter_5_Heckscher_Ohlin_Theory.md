7. State the Heckscher–Ohlin theorem and distinguish it from the Stolper–Samuelson theorem.

> Difficulty: Basic

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Heckscher–Ohlin predicts that a country exports the good that uses its abundant factor intensively. Stolper–Samuelson predicts how a change in a good’s relative price affects the real returns to factors.

8. What is the Leontief paradox?

> Difficulty: Basic

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

The finding that U.S. exports appeared less capital-intensive than U.S. import substitutes, contrary to the simple prediction that a capital-abundant country exports capital-intensive goods.
