4. Home needs 2 labour hours per unit of cloth and 4 hours per unit of food. Foreign needs 6 hours per unit of cloth and 3 hours per unit of food. Find each country’s opportunity cost of cloth and identify comparative advantage.

> Difficulty: Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Home’s opportunity cost is 2/4 = 0.5 units of food per cloth. Foreign’s is 6/3 = 2 units of food per cloth. Home has comparative advantage in cloth; Foreign has comparative advantage in food.

5. Why can a country with an absolute advantage in both goods still gain from trade?

> Difficulty: Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Absolute advantage does not determine relative opportunity costs. Specialising in the good with lower opportunity cost allows resources to be used more efficiently and expands consumption possibilities.
