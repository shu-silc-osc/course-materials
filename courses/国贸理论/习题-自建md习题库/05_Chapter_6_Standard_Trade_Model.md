9. Define the terms of trade and explain one channel through which a large-country import tariff may affect welfare.

> Difficulty: Basic

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

The terms of trade are the price of exports relative to imports. A large-country tariff may lower the foreign export price and improve the home terms of trade, but its production and consumption distortions may outweigh that gain.

10. Distinguish the production point from the consumption point in a trade diagram.

> Difficulty: Basic

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

The production point is where the relative-price line is tangent to the PPF; the consumption point is selected on the feasible trade-budget line according to preferences. They differ when the country trades.
