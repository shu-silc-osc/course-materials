6. When the relative price of a country’s export good rises, identify the likely short-run effects on labour allocation and the returns to specific factors.

> Difficulty: Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Labour moves toward the export industry. The specific factor in that industry gains; the specific factor in the import-competing industry loses. Workers’ real-wage effects depend on the goods they consume.
