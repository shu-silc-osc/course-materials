13. Why do only some firms export when trade barriers fall?

> Difficulty: Basic

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Firms differ in productivity and exporting has fixed costs. Only firms productive enough to cover those costs enter export markets.

14. What is intra-industry trade?

> Difficulty: Basic

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Two-way trade in differentiated products within the same industry, supported by economies of scale and consumers’ preference for variety.
