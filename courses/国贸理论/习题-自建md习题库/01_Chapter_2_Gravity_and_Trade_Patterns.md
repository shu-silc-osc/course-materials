# Chapter 2: Gravity and Trade Patterns

#### Multiple-Choice Questions

1. In the basic gravity model, bilateral trade tends to be larger when:
   A. both economies are smaller and farther apart  
   B. either economy is larger and the countries are closer  
   C. both countries impose higher tariffs  
   D. the countries have fewer transport links

> Difficulty: Basic

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

B.  GDP raises trade capacity and income; distance raises trade costs.

</details>

2. A country trades less than its GDP and distance predict. Which factor could explain the deviation?
   A. Shared language  B. A trade agreement  C. A border delay  D. A larger port network

> Difficulty: Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

C.  Border formalities and delays can reduce trade; the other choices generally facilitate it.

#### Short Answer

</details>

3. Why can a border reduce trade even when two countries share a language and have a trade agreement?

> Difficulty: Basic

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Customs procedures, time costs, currency conversion, and remaining tariffs or regulatory differences still raise the cost of cross-border exchange.
