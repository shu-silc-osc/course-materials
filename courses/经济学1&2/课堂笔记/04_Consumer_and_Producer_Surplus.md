# Chapter 4: Consumer and Producer Surplus

## 章节概览

消费者剩余与生产者剩余把市场交易带来的福利转化为可度量面积。竞争均衡在无市场失灵时最大化总剩余。

## 核心框架

- 消费者剩余 = 支付意愿 - 实际支付价格。
- 生产者剩余 = 实际收到价格 - 卖方成本。
- 总剩余 = 消费者剩余 + 生产者剩余；有效率数量使互利交易全部实现。

## 课堂要点

- Consumer and producer Surplus（消费者和生产者剩余）
### 1. the consumer surplus and its relationship to the demand curve

- (1) consumer surplus：the difference between market price and what consumers (as individuals or the market) would be willing to pay，消费者支付意愿和实际支付价 格的差额。
- (2) A consumer’s willingness to pay（支付意愿） for a good is the maximum price at which he or she would buy that good. （福利）
### 2. the producer surplus and its relationship to the supply curve

- (1) Producer surplus: the difference between market price and the price at which firms are willing to supply the product. 供给线----边际成本曲线 企业最优边界
### 3. the total surplus

- (1) Total surplus: the sum of the producer and consumer surpluses.
- (2) Three ways might to increase the total surplus a reallocate consumption among consumers（剩余减少，不成立） b reallocate sales among sellers（剩余减少，不成立） 这三种可能性均不成⽴ c change the quantity traded（剩余减少，不成立）
### 4. the efficiency of markets

- (1) They allocate consumption of the good to the potential buyers who most value it.（配置商品）
- (2) They allocate sales to the potential sellers who most value the right to sell the good (e.g., who have the lowest cost).（配置商品）
- (3) They ensure that all transactions（交易） are mutually beneficial: Every consumer who makes a purchase values the good more than every seller who makes a sale.
### 5. the reason why markets lead to efficient results

- (1) propety rights（Private property rights create and protect incentives to trade with others—and to innovate.） （产权界定）
- (2) economic signals （for producers） Prices translate complex information into an easy signal Competitive markets are usually efficient. market failure
