# Chapter 5: Price Controls and Quotas

## 章节概览

价格上限、价格下限和配额改变市场价格信号。政策可能重新分配剩余，同时造成交易量下降、资源错配、质量变化、寻租和黑市。

## 核心框架

- 有约束力的价格上限低于均衡价格并产生短缺。
- 有约束力的价格下限高于均衡价格并产生剩余。
- 配额租金等于需求价格与供给价格之间的楔子；交易量下降会产生无谓损失。

## 课堂要点

### 1. Two kinds of government intervention in markets: price controls and quantity controls

### 2. The problems caused by government intervention and the ways

- (1) Distorted price signals (价格信号扭曲) cause resources to be misallocated（资 源错配）
- (2) If prices are distorted, they cannot give good information to buyers and sellers.（信息扭曲、错误决策）
### 3. Price controls（价格管制）

- (1) price controls：legal restrictions on how high or low a market price may go（市场价格高低的法律限制）
- (2) Two main types：1）price ceiling 价格上限：a maximum price sellers are allowed to charge for a good or service (usually set BELOW equilibrium) 2）Price floor 价格下限: a minimum price buyers are required to pay for a good or service (usually set ABOVE equilibrium).
- (3) price ceiling 1）side effects：Inefficiently low quantity（shortages are created，成交量偏低导致 的低效率） Nonbinding price ceiling(无效的价格上限) binding or effective prive ceiling（有效的有约束性的价格上限） 经济信号--价格 Deadweight loss(净损失): the loss in total surplus that occurs whenever an action or a policy reduces the quantity transacted below the efficient market equilibrium quantity. Winners and losers from rent control Inefficient allocation to customers （消费机会的低效率配置）：Consumers who value a good most don’t necessarily get it.（价格信号扭曲，最需要商品的消费者不 一定能得到商品） wasted resources（资源浪费）：Price controls that create shortages lead to bribery and wasteful lines，例如，急着租房的人租不到，从而浪费时间、精力甚至金钱，造成资 源浪费。
- inefficiently low quality（低质量导致的低效率）：Because of the lower price, there is no need for sellers to improve quality and service. black markets（黑市）: a market in which goods or services are bought and sold illegally—either because they are prohibited or because the equilibrium price is illegal. 2) the reasons why the price controls occur:benefit some people (who are typically better organized and more vocal than those who are harmed by them)，政府不仅要 追求效率，还要尽量保证公平。
- (4) price floor（eg.最低工资标准） side effect：1）Deadweight loss from inefficiency low quantity 2）inefficient allocation of sales among sellers（销售机会的错配，部分成本低、竞争 力强的商家由于信息原因，卖不出去商品） 3）Wasted resources（卖不出去商品的商家会增加营销费用，造成浪费；
- 商品浪费） 4）inefficiently high quality（商家发展无效的竞争） 5）black market（黑市）：temptation to break the law by selling below the legal price
### 3. controlling quantities（数量控制）

- (1) Quota（配额）: an upper limit, set by the government, on the quantity of some good that can be bought or sold; also referred to as a quantity control.（对可 买卖数量设定的上限） Quota limit（限额）: the total amount of a good under a quota or quantity control that can be legally transacted.（一般低于均衡成交量） License（许可证）: the right, conferred by the government, to supply a good. Demand price: the price of a given quantity at which consumers will demand that quantity. Supply price: the price of a given quantity at which producers will supply that quantity. The wedge，or quota，rent（配额租金，政府拿走的部分）
- (2) side effects：1）Deadweight loss 2）Incentives for illegal activities
### 4. The similarity between price ceilings，floors and quotas：all decrease the amount traded and therefore create deadweight loss. 1）A price ceiling pushes the price of a good down; fewer sellers will want to sell. 2）A price floor pushes the price of a good up; fewer buyers will want to buy. 3）A quota, by definition, reduces sales.
